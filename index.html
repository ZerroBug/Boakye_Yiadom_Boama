In honour of · Profile

Mr. Boakye
Yiadom Boama

A life shaped by humble beginnings, education, service, leadership and a deep commitment to Catholic values.

Mr. Boakye Yiadom Boama was born on 21 October 1966 to the late Maame Afia Pomaa of Nsuta Asuafu and the late Papa Akwasi Tetewu Boateng of Sekyere Beposo. He is the third of nine siblings, married to Mrs. Hanna Adu Boakye, and a father of eight children.

His educational journey began at Asuafu Salvation Army Primary School, continued through Asuafu L/A Middle School and Nsutaman Catholic Secondary School, and later led him to Tweneboa Kodua Secondary School for sixth-form education.

A distinguished journey

Education, leadership
& service.

The official profile records a career spanning classroom teaching, school administration, educational research, examination service and community leadership.

1971 – 1983

Foundations in Education

Asuafu Salvation Army Primary School, Asuafu L/A Middle School and Nsutaman Catholic Secondary School.

1984 – 1986

Sixth Form Education

Tweneboa Kodua Secondary School, where he obtained the G.C.E. 'A' Level Certificate.

1989 – 1997

University of Ghana & University of Cape Coast

B.A. Hons in Political Science with Philosophy, followed by a Post Graduate Diploma of Education.

1993 – 2011

Teaching & School Leadership

Served at Yeji Secondary Technical School and Seventh-Day Adventist Senior Secondary School at Agona, taking on teaching and leadership responsibilities.

2011 – 2019

Senior Administration

Served as Assistant Headmaster at Konadu Yiadom SHS and Konongo-Odumase SHS, and later as substantive Headmaster of Denyaeman Catholic SHS.

17 December 2019

Okomfo Anokye Senior High School

Reassigned to Okomfo Anokye Senior High School as substantive Headmaster, a responsibility recorded in the profile as continuing up to date.

Academic Qualifications

  • B.A. Hons, Political Science with Philosophy — University of Ghana, 1992.
  • Post Graduate Diploma of Education — University of Cape Coast, completed 1997.
  • M.Ed. Educational Administration — University of Cape Coast, completed 2015.

Ranks Held

1993Superintendent1996Senior Superintendent2001Principal Superintendent2006Assistant Director II2010Assistant Director I2015Deputy Director

Research & Publications

Three unpublished dissertations are recorded in the official profile, including work on local government administration, teaching Government in Senior Secondary Schools, and supervision of teaching and learning.

Publications: Millennium Government for Senior Secondary Schools and Millennium Government — Questions and Answers.

Community & Examination Service

Community service includes leadership roles at Legon Hall Annex B and SDA Senior High School Agona-Ashanti, as well as NAGRAT Chairman for the Agona Zone. The profile also records WAEC/WASSCE supervision from 2011 and examiner/vetter service for Government from 2005–2024.
